What We Do

End-to-end capability across MOD and Government programmes, from initial concept through to fielded delivery. Grounded in operational experience, executed with technical precision.

Consulting & Strategy

Operational insight and strategic advisory for defence acquisition, capability development, and programme delivery, grounded in genuine front-line experience.

Rapid Prototyping

From concept to fielded prototype at pace. Physical and digital demonstrators built to prove capability early and reduce procurement risk.

Software Development

Bespoke software for complex operational environments, including simulation tooling, data pipelines, mobile applications, and embedded systems.

Hardware & Integration

Custom hardware design, procurement, and system integration, connecting physical assets to digital networks across training and operational platforms.

Simulation & Modelling

High-fidelity simulation environments, verified unit model data sets, and synthetic entity creation built from real-world order-of-battle data.

Training Delivery

Full lifecycle training system delivery, from hardware procurement through exercise support, instructor tools, and post-exercise analysis.
