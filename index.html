What We Do

End-to-end capability across Defence and Government programmes - grounded in operational understanding, executed with technical precision.

Consulting & Capability Development

Requirements capture, capability development, and programme support informed by genuine operational experience and engineering expertise.

Rapid Prototyping

Physical and software prototyping at pace. CAD design, 3D printing, proof-of-concept software, and capability demonstrators at a fraction of the cost of traditional defence contractors.

Software Engineering

Bespoke defence and Government software including mobile applications, operational tools, data pipelines, and capability demonstrators with minimal overhead and direct engineer access.

Simulation & Modelling

Simulation solutions using established defence platforms including MASA SWORD, VBS4, CAW GESI, and JCAST. Data collection, model implementation, scenario generation, and 3D asset creation.

Hardware & Integration

Hardware procurement, installation, integration, and deployment across VR, AR, and simulation platforms including full training environment setup and configuration.

Training Delivery

Full lifecycle training system delivery - from hardware procurement and installation through exercise planning, live delivery, and post-exercise analysis.
