Capabilities

What We Do

01

Consulting & Strategy

Strategic advisory for defence acquisition, programme scoping, and capability development. Our consultants have held operational roles and understand the constraints, pressures, and priorities that drive MOD and Government decision-making.

We provide independent, mission-aligned recommendations, from feasibility assessments and options analysis through to delivery strategy and supplier engagement.

Typical Deliverables

Capability Assessments Options Analysis Requirements Scoping Programme Advisory Supplier Engagement
02

Rapid Prototyping

From concept to fielded prototype at pace. We compress the gap between operational requirement and physical demonstrator, building hardware, software, and hybrid solutions that prove capability before full investment is committed.

Our approach is iterative and transparent, with regular client touchpoints so that the prototype reflects the reality of the requirement, not just the initial specification.

Typical Deliverables

Physical Demonstrators Software Prototypes Test & Evaluation Proof of Concept Reports
03

Software Engineering

Bespoke software built for complex, constrained operational environments. We develop mobile applications, simulation tooling, data pipelines, and embedded systems to defence-grade standards, with full documentation and handover packages.

Our software team has delivered live products for Government and MOD clients, including field-deployed applications, XML data tooling, and synthetic training systems.

Typical Deliverables

Mobile Applications Simulation Tooling Data Pipelines Embedded Systems API Integration
04

Hardware & Integration

Custom hardware design, procurement, and system integration. We connect physical assets to digital networks, configure training hardware, and manage the full integration process from acceptance testing through to fielded deployment.

Typical Deliverables

Hardware Design Procurement & Configuration System Integration Acceptance Testing Deployment Support
05

Simulation & Modelling

High-fidelity simulation environments and verified unit model data sets built from real-world order-of-battle data. We support platforms including MASA SWORD and VBS4, delivering synthetic entities, force structures, and exercise configurations specific to the client's use case.

Typical Deliverables

Unit Model Data Sets 3D Entity Models Exercise Configuration MASA SWORD VBS4
06

Training Delivery

Full lifecycle training system delivery, from hardware procurement through exercise planning, live delivery, and after-action review support. We work with unit training teams to reduce their burden, not add to it, providing instructor tools, scenario packages, and post-exercise analysis.

Typical Deliverables

Exercise Planning Scenario Packages Instructor Tooling VR / AR Systems Post-Exercise Analysis
