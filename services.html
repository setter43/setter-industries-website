Consulting & Capability Development

We bridge the gap between front-line operational understanding and technical delivery, providing requirements capture, capability development, options analysis, and programme support that places the mission requirement above all else.

Rapid Prototyping

Physical and software prototyping delivered at pace with a commercially efficient SME model. We leverage a lean SME model with minimal overhead to move from concept to working demonstrator rapidly.

Software prototyping covers application development, software proof-of-concepts, capability demonstrators, and user-interface development - enabling requirements validation before full programme investment is committed.
