Who We Are

Setter Industries was established to bring genuine operational and technical understanding to the Defence and Government technology market. Over 50% of our team are veterans of the British Armed Forces, with careers spanning active service, operational deployments, and specialist roles within the MOD.

That combination of lived operational experience and modern software engineering capability is what sets Setter Industries apart. We understand the environments our solutions will be used in, because we have operated within them.
