Who We Are

Setter Industries was established to bring genuine operational understanding to the defence technology market. Over 50% of our team are veterans of the British Armed Forces, with service careers that include active deployments, large-scale exercises across the globe, and specialist technical roles within the MOD.

That lived experience, on the ground, under pressure, with the tools and systems that actually matter, is what separates us from conventional technology consultancies. We don't interpret operational requirements. We've lived them.

The Team

Leadership

Ben Taylor
Director & Lead Developer

Ben founded Setter Industries on the premise that the best defence technology is built by people who understand the operational environment it will be used in. A former Royal Engineer with six years of Regular service including one operational tour and multiple large-scale exercises across the globe, Ben subsequently served in the Army Reserve Officer Training Corps and as a Regular Reservist in Royal Engineer EOD.

Alongside his military career, Ben holds a degree in Computer Science and has spent 4.5 years delivering projects across software engineering, hardware, and simulation for Government and MOD clients, successfully taking complex programmes from requirements through to live delivery.
