Governance

Company Policies

Policy Framework

Lightweight, credible and maintained

Our policies are proportionate to a specialist SME and are reviewed as the business, customer base and contractual obligations develop.

Quality Policy

Work is planned against agreed requirements and acceptance criteria. Deliverables are reviewed, tested and controlled before release. Lessons learned are retained and used to improve future delivery.

Information Security Policy

Information is protected according to sensitivity, contractual requirements and business risk through access control, secure devices, approved storage and incident response.

Artificial Intelligence Use Policy

AI-assisted tools may be used to improve productivity where appropriate. Human review remains mandatory, and restricted customer information is never submitted to public systems without authorisation.

Equality and Inclusion

Setter Industries supports fair treatment, professional conduct and decisions based on capability, suitability and the needs of the work.

Anti-Bribery and Conflicts

Bribery, improper influence and undisclosed conflicts of interest are not accepted. Potential conflicts are identified and managed transparently.

Environmental Responsibility

We seek to reduce avoidable travel, waste, energy use and unnecessary hardware consumption while meeting customer and security requirements.

Privacy Notice

We process contact and business information supplied through enquiries, procurement and project activity for legitimate business, contractual and legal purposes. Requests can be sent to privacy@setterindustries.co.uk.

Cookie Notice

This static website does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies. Third-party content or hosting services may process basic technical information required to deliver the site.

Formal policy documents

Signed or customer-specific policy statements, certificates and evidence can be supplied during onboarding or tender response where relevant.
